# Greenstone Drinking Game

Greenstone's traditional game is played by two people. The challenger chooses equal cups for both (traditionally four); the challenged chooses a drink available locally and no higher in rank than the lower-ranked player. Take turns asking a question. Answer honestly or drink; there is no magical truth enforcement. Spectators may cheer or boo, but this has no automatic dice effect. The first person to empty their cups or vomit loses.

| Drink size | Cups used | Base CON save DC |
|---|---:|---:|
| Small sip | 1/4 | 8 |
| Normal gulp | 1/2 | 11 |
| Large gulp | 1 | 14 |
| Show-off gulp | 2 | 18 |

You cannot drink more cups than remain. On each drink, roll a Constitution save at **base DC + drink modifier**. There is no additional rank difference modifier. Success adds 0 intoxication; failure adds 1; failure by 5 or more adds 2 total. At 2+ points, subsequent **main** drinking saves have disadvantage. Starting at 3 points, make a separate vomiting Constitution save on **every** drink, including the drink that first reaches 3, even if its main save succeeded. Its DC is **10 + 2 x current points**. It does **not** have disadvantage from intoxication. Failure means vomit and lose. Empty cups also means lose. No exhaustion, Poisoned condition, or passing-out rule applies.
