# Essence Basics

Essences are supernatural powers that shape a person's body, abilities, instincts, and identity.

Most essence users combine three essences into a confluence.

This file only includes essence information the players currently need or have discovered.

## Essence Points

Essence powers use Essence Points. Confluence powers are separate.

- Each character has Essence Points equal to half their character level, rounded down.
- Using an essence power costs 1 Essence Point.
- Spent Essence Points reset to maximum after a long rest.

## Known Player Confluence Powers

### Astral Forge

#### Astral Forge

Once per long rest, you channel astral energy into a target through consecutive successful strikes.

#### Consecutive Hits

- 1st hit: normal damage + 1 thunder damage.
- 2nd hit: normal damage + 2 thunder damage.
- 3rd hit: normal damage + 3 thunder damage.
- Continue increasing by +1 thunder damage per consecutive hit.

The bonus thunder damage cannot exceed your level.

#### Special Property

- Thunder resistance functions normally.
- Thunder immunity is treated as thunder resistance.

#### Portable Smithy

You can manifest a complete mystical forge that only you can operate. You can use it to repair equipment, forge mundane and magical items, and work metal anywhere, provided you have the necessary materials.

#### Ore Sense

You can instinctively sense the presence of rare metals, valuable minerals, and magical ores nearby or underground. You do not automatically know the exact type or quantity, only that something valuable is present.

#### Resonance Detonation

If you miss an attack after reaching at least 3 consecutive successful hits:

- The accumulated energy detonates.
- Damage: your level x number of consecutive hits.
- Damage type: Transcended Damage.
- Transcended Damage ignores resistance and immunity.
- The effect then ends.

### Catalyst

#### Catalytic Domain

Once per long rest, manipulate ambient magic in a 15-foot radius for 1 minute.

Choose one of the following effects when activating the domain.

#### Vital Surge

- Allies gain temporary HP equal to your level x 5.
- This does not stack with existing temporary HP.
- Temporary HP from this effect is lost upon leaving the area.

#### Entropic Surge

- Enemies ending their turn in the area must make a Constitution saving throw.
- On a failure, they take necrotic damage equal to your level.
- On a success, they take half damage.

#### Special Property

- The necrotic damage ignores necrotic resistance.
- Necrotic immunity is treated as necrotic resistance.

#### Magical Stability Sense

You can naturally sense the stability of magic around you, unless it has been intentionally concealed. You instinctively perceive stable magic, unstable magic, magic on the verge of collapse, corrupted structures, and dangerous fluctuations.

This does not reveal the cause, only that something is wrong.

#### Stabilizing Presence

Magic has a much harder time going wild or destabilizing around you. Rituals, spells, ambient magic, and magical devices are more stable.

Exception: if you become involved in an ongoing magical transformation, your presence can alter the process itself.

### Nova

#### Solar Aegis

Once per long rest, you become a vessel of stellar light and unyielding heat.

Choose one of the following effects when activating Solar Aegis.

#### Solar Retribution

Whenever an enemy misses a melee attack against you, the attacker must make a Dexterity saving throw.

The DC equals your level + 10.

- Failure: the attacker suffers fire damage equal to your level and radiant damage equal to your level.
- Success: the attacker suffers half of both damage types, rounded up.

#### Blazing Challenge

As a reaction after an enemy declares an attack against you but before the attack roll is made, you may reduce your AC by 2 until the start of your next turn.

While this penalty is active, all fire and radiant damage dealt by Solar Retribution is doubled.

#### Light of Recovery

When not in combat, sick creatures near the user gain advantage on checks or saving throws made to overcome illness and recover.

#### Sense Malice

The user can instinctively sense malicious intent from creatures of a lower rank.

- Detects intent, not thoughts.
- Does not reveal who is malicious if multiple creatures are present.
- Does not trigger on friendly sparring or harmless pranks.
- Works only against creatures of a lower rank.
- A skilled higher-ranked individual can conceal hostile intent.

### Twilight

#### Shadow Harvest

Once per long rest, choose a number of enemies up to your level.

Those targets gain a Shadow Parasite for 1 minute.

#### Shadow Resonance

When you damage a creature carrying a Shadow Parasite:

- The target takes additional cold damage equal to your level.
- The additional cold damage ignores cold resistance.
- Cold immunity is treated as cold resistance.

#### Spreading Twilight

All other creatures carrying a Shadow Parasite must make a Dexterity saving throw.

- On failure, they take cold damage equal to your level.
- On success, they take no damage.

#### Special Properties

Primary Target, meaning the creature you damage directly:

- Cold damage ignores cold resistance.
- Cold immunity is treated as cold resistance.

Secondary Targets, meaning creatures affected by Spreading Twilight:

- Cold resistance functions normally.
- Cold immunity is treated as cold resistance.

#### Shadow Communion

You can interact peacefully and nonviolently with shadows. You can touch, sense, and communicate with them in limited ways, and manipulate them without expending magic.

#### Detached Shadow

You can detach your own shadow from your body. It can move independently and perform one simple task at a time, but it cannot attack or directly harm creatures.

It remains linked to you.

Examples: retrieve an item, pull a lever, open an unlocked door, deliver a message, tap someone's shoulder, hold a lantern, press a button, and similar simple tasks.

#### Shadows Greed

A semi-reliable looting power. You are able to loot dead creatures of your rank or higher. The stronger the Will the creature had in life, the higher the chance of loot.

- Roll 1d100 when attempting to loot.
- The DM assigns a Will modifier based on the creature's will in life.
- Add the modifier to your roll.
- The higher the total, the greater the chance of obtaining magical loot.
- The Will of the creature is decided by the DM and adds a number to the player's 1d100 roll for loot.
