# Known Organizations

Organizations the party has encountered or learned about.

## Adventurers Association

The Adventurers Association handles adventuring teams, ranks, quests, field tests, and Bronze promotion.

See [Party Reference](Party%20Reference.md) for known team details.

## Magic Association

The Magic Association is connected to magic research, regulation, and the Church of Knowledge.

Players may know it as an authority on magical study, magical law, magical theory, enchanting, alchemy, and dangerous magical incidents.

Niles joined the Magic Association during Sessions 01-02.

## Churches

Several churches are publicly known and tied to the major gods.

Known examples:

- Church of Knowledge
- Church of Journey
- Church of Healer
- Church of Purity
- Church of Light

Father Tobias Crowe of the Church of Light signed official papers placing Ron and Don under Church of Light protection.
